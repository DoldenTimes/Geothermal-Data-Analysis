## CLAUDE.md rules as applied

- `README.md`, `Logs/` and earlier results in git history were ignored; everything was rebuilt from `Baseline/`, which was only read (`git status Baseline` shows no change).
- Scripts are in `Scripts/`; outputs only in `Output/Calibration_Input/` (report, five target tables, figure index, standard figures F1-F7) and `Output/Additional_Output/` (everything else). Nothing is written loose in `Output/`.
- Scope is the natural state; production, injection and gauge records were used only for the natural-state timing test (§4.1) and for F7.
- Inventory first (§2.1): every Baseline file assigned a role by content/location rules in the script; reference-model files classified but not read as evidence.
- Default methods, thresholds, weights and figure layouts of §4, §5 and §8 are used in the standard columns and figures; extras are added as extra columns (`near_boiling`, `deep_fit_n`, `deep_fit_span_m`, `water_level_extrapolation_m`, `regime_note`, along-hole gauge columns, etc.) or extra files.
- IAPWS-IF97 via the `iapws` package internals (Region 4 `_TSat_P`/`_PSat_T`, Region 1 `_Region1`), verified in the script against the IF97 verification tables and the two §4.3 reference values; the script exits if any Tsat error exceeds 0.1 K (largest error 0.0043 K).
- Claim tags `[obs]`, `[interp]`, `[assumption]`, `[reference model]` used throughout the report; every report number is read from an output table (check R1: 0 unmatched).

## Steps

2. Checked §3 format statements against the files:
   - Layer, surface and well sections parse by whitespace and the layers tile with no gaps: holds.
   - **Does not hold:** the `VERTICES` section is fixed-width with no separating whitespace (e.g. `aab2812872.706327567.88`). A fixed-width regex is used for that section only (departure 1 below).
   - Feed-zone units `m` and track units `masl` are elevations in mRL: holds (feed-zone mids coincide with survey points; tracks match the grid file's WELLS section).
   - Transient `.dat` times are years from 2014-01-01, in nominal monthly steps of 1/12 year (max 0.004 year from exact dates). Production rates are negative in `.dat`, positive in JSON.
   - Irregular `.dat` names: `Wel10_*` (matches AUT2 name `WEL10`); matched to WELL-10 by content.
3. Wrote the script in stages: inventory and parsing → integrity checks → IF97 → natural-state test → weights and tables → §4.4 summaries → hydrostatic windows → sections and figures → conceptual model → report.

## Independent review (try to disprove each conclusion)

- "Deep pressures agree within a few bar below -500 mRL" — **false**: WELL-8 is +10.3 bar off-trend at -2000 mRL (cold, dense column) and WELL-1 -13.1 bar at -500 mRL. Rewrote the paragraph using the -1000 mRL values and the column-temperature explanation.
- "Open-hole windows in hot wells are close to the cold-well component" — WELL-2 open hole is 1.0921; rewritten to give the range and name WELL-2.
- "Deeper heat source" below inversions (regime notes) — overclaims; the deep gradients are only 7–14 degC/km. Reworded to "rises again slowly".
- Conclusion 4 listed WELL-8's deep gradient among outflow wells — restricted to outflow wells.
- "Upflow between Fault 1 and Fault 3" — reworded to "near", with both distances, and the fault-path claim tagged [interp].
- WELL-2's rule-based regime is "outflow" but it is used as an upflow well in the conceptual model; the disagreement is stated in `regime_note`, not in `regime`.
- Conceptual element 8 claim type was "mixed" (not an allowed report tag); changed to interp.
- The geometry sentence in the report was asserted, not computed; it now counts agreements from `well_geometry_check.csv`.
- Survey-date conclusion: tested against the alternative that surveys were taken on the label date — the gauges had already fallen 9.1 and 8.2 bar by 2015-01-01, so a survey taken on 2015-07-29 could not match the 2014 values. Conclusion stands.

## Departures from the instructions

1. **Vertices parsed fixed-width** (§3 says parse by whitespace): the section has no whitespace between fields, so whitespace parsing is impossible. Only that section; layers, surface and wells are whitespace-parsed.
2. **Hydrostatic windows that span a casing shoe have a blank `band`** (with `band_note`). The §4.3 window rule (close at a feature only once ≥300 m; merge a short remnant upward) produces 9 such windows; they belong to neither band as defined, so they are counted only in the overall statistics. No cold-well window lies wholly behind casing, so the cased-band cold-well component is undefined.
3. **Section figures show the line cropped to the wells ±2000 m**; the line itself still runs to the model extent (full end coordinates in titles, report and `section_lines.csv`), and edge labels give the distance to each end. Without cropping the 13–15 km lines left the wells unreadable.
4. **Three F4 lines** (A through the two hottest wells, plus B and C chosen automatically as the lines capturing most wells within 500 m). F6 uses line B (most wells); conceptual sections on A and C are extra figures in `Additional_Output/figures/`.
5. Interpretive choices not fixed by CLAUDE.md, stated in the report and `parameters_used.csv`: a gauge reading on the day production starts counts as pre-production [assumption]; injectate temperature from enthalpy at 10 bar [assumption]; "near the injectate temperature" = within 10 degC; cap base = first crossing of 0.9 × Tmax; upflow well = regime upflow or T at TD ≥ 0.9 × field Tmax; F3 band means are simple means of survey points, band membership bottom ≤ z < top, `P_minus_trend_bar` is the mean of point residuals.
6. No reference-model companion section (§8.3 is optional).
